Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about using AssanPay, from setup to settlement everything you need to get started. Have a question or need assistance? Contact AssanPay Payment Gateway our support team is here to help you.

What is AssanPay?

AssanPay is a leading Payment Gateway Pakistan solution that enables businesses to collect online payments through multiple channels, including bank transfers, cards (Visa & Mastercard), and mobile wallets like Easypaisa, JazzCash, and others. Designed for security and simplicity, AssanPay helps merchants accept payments instantly and manage transactions effortlessly all within a trusted digital ecosystem.

Who can use AssanPay?

Any registered business, freelancer, or e-commerce platform in Pakistan can use AssanPay to collect customer payments quickly and securely.

What payment methods does AssanPay support?

  • Visa & Mastercard

  • Easypaisa

  • JazzCash

  • Upaisa

  • 1LINK

  • Zindigi

  • Raast QR
How quickly are payments settled?

Most payments are settled within 1 working day; however, the exact settlement timeline may vary based on the merchant’s agreement, transaction volume, and the payment method selected. All terms are clearly defined at the time of onboarding.

Is AssanPay secure?

Yes, AssanPay follows industry-standard security protocols, including OTP verification, encrypted transactions, and secure redirection for every payment.

How to delete your account (in the app)
  1. Open the AssanPay app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm, select a reason for leaving, and submit your request.


You will be signed out on all devices immediately. Your account is then scheduled for permanent closure after a 30-day grace period. If you change your mind, simply sign back in within those 30 days and choose Reactivate, your account will be fully restored. After 30 days the closure is permanent.
