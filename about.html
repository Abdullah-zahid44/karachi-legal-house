About Karachi Legal House

سندھ ہائی کورٹ، سپریم کورٹ اور سیشن عدالتوں میں 24 سالہ بلاشرکتِ غیرے عدالتی کارکردگی

24 Years of Judicial Representation & Ethics

Founded in 2002 by Senior Advocate Mr. Shamsuddin Rajper, former Deputy Attorney General for Pakistan and Ex-Vice President of the Sindh High Court Bar Association (Sukkur Bench), Karachi Legal House has maintained an unblemished record of judicial advocacy.

Operating from Karachi, our firm serves as standing legal counsel for major financial institutions, state authorities, and corporate entities while offering compassionate, robust representation in criminal, civil, and family litigation.

"Ethics, statutory precision, and relentless trial preparation define our duty to the court and to our clients."
