Overview

Dedicated Family & Custody Law Counsel in Karachi

Family cases are decided as much in chambers as in courtrooms. Our family practice handles khula, divorce documentation, child custody and maintenance with discretion — pushing for dignified settlement where possible, and litigating firmly in the Family Courts of Karachi where necessary.

We act for wives seeking khula and husbands defending their rights alike, and we never lose sight of the court's guiding principle in custody matters: the welfare of the minor is paramount. From the Family Court plaint to the Union Council effectiveness certificate, we manage the complete paper trail.

Common Questions

Case ki noiyat par munhasir hai, lekin aam tor par chand mah me decree ho jati hai. Decree ke baad 90 din me Union Council se effectiveness certificate lena lazmi hota hai.

Adalat 'bachay ki falaah' (welfare of minor) ko sab se upar rakhti hai — umar, dekh-bhaal, taleem aur mahol sab dekha jata hai. Doosre waledain ko visitation schedule milta hai.

Shohar ka inkar khula ko rok nahi sakta — adalat biwi ke bayan aur saboot par decree de sakti hai. Albatta haq mehr aur saman ki wapsi ke daway alag se chalte hain.

Adalat interim maintenance muqadma ke doran bhi muqarrar kar sakti hai. Final maintenance biwi aur nabaligh bachon dono ke liye ho sakti hai.

Ji — Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ke tehat talaq ka tehreeri notice Union Council ko bhejna aur 90 din ka reconciliation period poora karna lazmi hai, warna talaq moassar tasawwur nahi hoti.
