Overview

Dedicated Property & Succession Counsel in Karachi

In Karachi, a property dispute is almost always a paperwork dispute — double selling, stay orders on 'clear' plots, lease titles that were never transferred. As property lawyers in Karachi, our practice starts where the fraud starts: verification. Before you pay a token, we verify title at the sub-registrar, check mutation and fard records, and confirm the lease chain with the relevant authority (KDA, KMC, DHA, Cantonment or cooperative society).

We also handle the other end of property law: succession certificates through NADRA's facilitation unit and letters of administration from the Sindh High Court, rent and eviction matters under the Sindh Rented Premises Ordinance, and possession and partition suits.

How We Work

Your Case, Step by Step

1

Document Collection

You share the file, allotment letter or registry copy — we list every missing link in the chain the same day.

2

Record Verification

Physical verification at the sub-registrar and the leasing authority; encumbrance and litigation search included.

3

Transaction or Filing

Clean title: we draft and register the transfer. Disputed title: suit, injunction or complaint filed immediately.

4

Mutation & Transfer

We follow through to intiqal/mutation and updated records — a file isn't closed until the record reflects you.

Common Questions

Sub-registrar record (ki jayedad farokht-kunanda ke naam hai), fard, mutation/intiqal, lease authority (KDA/DHA/Cantonment) ka record, aur koi stay order ya qarza (encumbrance) to nahi. Token se pehle ye sab lazmi hai.

Allotment/lease authority se duplicate file aur sub-registrar se certified copies nikalwai ja sakti hain. Hum poora retrieval process karwate hain.

Wursa me ittefaq ho to NADRA succession certificate; ikhtilaf ho to Sindh High Court se letter of administration. Phir mutation karwa kar record update hota hai.

Sindh Rented Premises Ordinance ke tehat eviction petition dayer hoti hai — wajooh (masalan zati zaroorat, kiraya adaigi me kotahi) ke saath. Ghalat tareeqe se zabardasti nikalna qanoon-shikan hai.

Fori stay/injunction ke liye civil suit aur forged documents par criminal complaint — dono karwai ikathi chalti hain. Der na karein.

NADRA ke Succession Facilitation Unit me darkhwast di jati hai — death certificate, warison ki tafseel aur biometric tasdeeq ke saath. NADRA movable assets ke liye succession certificate aur immovable property ke liye letter of administration dono jari karta hai. Warison me ittefaq ho to certificate aam tor par chand hafton me mil jata hai; ikhtilaf ya decline ki surat me adalat jana parta hai.
