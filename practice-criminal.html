Overview

Dedicated Criminal Defense & Bail Counsel in Karachi

A criminal case in Karachi moves fast — and the first 48 hours often decide its direction. Karachi Legal House defends clients from the police-station stage through trial and appeal, with a practice built around bail strategy, FIR quashment and rigorous trial defense in the Sessions Courts of Karachi and the Sindh High Court.

Our advocates regularly handle pre-arrest bail petitions under Section 498 CrPC, post-arrest bail under Section 497 CrPC, quashment of mala fide FIRs, and full trial defense under the Pakistan Penal Code — including white-collar matters before NAB and FIA special courts. Every case begins with a confidential assessment of the FIR, the evidence on record, and the realistic forums available to you.

What We Handle

Services Under This Practice

Pre-Arrest Bail (498 CrPC)

Protective bail before arrest in false or motivated FIRs — drafted, filed and argued on urgency in the Sessions Court.

Post-Arrest Bail (497 CrPC)

Bail after arrest on statutory and discretionary grounds, with surety arrangement and compliance guidance.

FIR Quashment

Quashment of mala fide FIRs through constitutional petitions and Section 561-A CrPC before the Sindh High Court.

Criminal Trial Defense

Complete trial conduct — charge framing, cross-examination, defense evidence and final arguments.

NAB & White-Collar Defense

Representation in NAB references, FIA inquiries and financial-crime investigations with parallel bail strategy.

Appeals & Revisions

Criminal appeals, revisions and jail petitions before the Sindh High Court against conviction or sentence.

How We Work

Your Case, Step by Step

1

Confidential Case Assessment

We study the FIR, recovery memos and remand papers, then give you an honest read of exposure and options — before you spend a rupee.

2

Bail & Protection Strategy

Where liberty is at stake, bail comes first: pre-arrest or post-arrest petitions filed on urgency with complete surety paperwork.

3

Trial Defense

Meticulous cross-examination, objections on inadmissible evidence, and defense witnesses — built around creating reasonable doubt.

4

Appeal & Closure

If convicted, immediate appeal and suspension of sentence; if acquitted, we close the paper trail so the case stays closed.

Common Questions

Pre-arrest bail (Section 498 CrPC) giraftari se pehle hifazati zamanat hai — jab FIR kat chuki ho lekin aap giraftar na hue hon. Post-arrest bail (Section 497 CrPC) giraftari ke baad milti hai. Dono me adalat jurm ki noiyat, saboot aur aap ke record ko dekhti hai.

Urgency par pre-arrest bail aksar chand din me sun li jati hai; post-arrest bail ka schedule adalat ke cause list par munhasir hai. Hum interim relief ke saath file karte hain taake giraftari ka khatra fori tal jaye.

Jhooti ya bad-niyyati par mabni FIR ko Sindh High Court me quash karwaya ja sakta hai (constitutional petition / 561-A CrPC). Is ke liye FIR, gawahon ke bayanat aur waqea ki timeline darkar hoti hai.

Surety ki raqam jurm aur adalat ke order par munhasir hai — adalat surety bonds aur tasdeeq-shuda documents mangti hai. Hum surety ka poora paperwork pehle se tayyar karwate hain taake rehai me der na ho.

Ji — agar mulzim adalat me pesh na ho, gawahon ko dhamkaye ya zamanat ki sharaait tode to prosecution cancellation maang sakti hai. Hum clients ko sharaait ki pabandi ka poora schedule samjhate hain.
